> **Overhaul:** 2026-11-07 (one hundred and twentieth pass) — **SC-56 P4: report-date alerts and daily digest (v1.105.0).** (1) New monitor check `report_dates` (domain \"report dates\", so the message reads \"FT report dates: Report dates looks down\"), evaluated from `ticker_calendar` over active stock holdings and non-deleted stock-watchlist rows, and ticked right after every hourly calendar pass (and by the 10-minute monitor, and listed in Diagnostics). It goes down when a ticker has: a fetch error that is a 404 (permanent, at once) or has lasted 3 consecutive hourly runs (a transient 429 or network blip does not alert); no calendar row at all; or a DATE_KNOWN / DATE_ESTIMATED date that is already in the past. NOT_APPLICABLE and NOT_ANNOUNCED are genuine states and never alert. It is not judged until some row has been updated in the last 6 hours, so a fresh deploy or a stopped job is not reported as missing dates. The monitor's own rules give one message when it goes down naming the tickers and states (at most eight per group), a reminder every 24 hours while it lasts, one recovery message, and nothing when clean. (2) The daily digest (`monitor.CalendarDigest`, 07:30 UTC) lists report-date changes of the last 24 hours for owned and watchlist tickers (changed date, new date found after a report, or date gone), marks estimates, ignores thesis-only tickers, is sent at most once per UTC day (table `ticker_calendar_digest_sent`, migration 0069, so a restart does not repeat it) and sends nothing when there is nothing to say. First population of a ticker and the backfill are not changes. (3) Sender: the same one SC-55 settled on, the monitor's Telegram sender (`FT_TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN` / `FT_TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID` in `/etc/ft/env`), not `/api/bot/alerts` as the handover named. Checked on Jarvis on 2026-11-07 without printing any secret: its bot token and chat id equal those in `/etc/ft-bot/env` (hash and string comparison), it is `@FinsFTAlerts_bot` (id 8955129725), and `getChat` on the chat succeeds and reports a private chat; no test message was sent. Tests (scratch DB, fake notifier): a clean day is silent; an injected 404 gives one alert, none on the next hour, one recovery message, none afterwards; a 429 needs three runs; a past date shown as next and a ticker with no row are reported; not judged before the job has run; the digest is sent once, ignores thesis-only tickers and is silent on a quiet day; the full job-to-alert path with a fake source; each rule was kill-tested. Migration: 0069. Not changed: backfill report (P5). Reference updated: §3, §4, §5. Supersedes v1.104.0.

(3) The breaker is ADVISORY: arming sets `risk_circuit_breaker_active` and `risk_circuit_breaker_until` (14 days) and sends ONE Telegram message on the transition to armed ("FT risk (ADVISORY): drawdown ... Auto-clear is unchanged (drawdown above -5% and the 14 days passed). (4) The Settings card shows "(ADVISORY)" next to the state, "BREACH, not yet armed" when the drawdown is beyond the cap but the flag is not set, and the basis under the drawdown figure. Behaviour: job, API, UI. Migration: 0073. Supersedes v1.111.0.
