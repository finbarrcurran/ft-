# FT spec archive — change log, part 07

Full, unedited change-log entries of the FT master spec, oldest first (this file only ever grows at the end; existing text is never edited — a correction goes in a new entry). Map of every version and pass to its file: INDEX.md.

> **Overhaul:** 2026-11-07 (one hundred and twentieth pass) — **SC-56 P4: report-date alerts and daily digest (v1.105.0).** (1) New monitor check `report_dates` (domain \"report dates\", so the message reads \"FT report dates: Report dates looks down\"), evaluated from `ticker_calendar` over active stock holdings and non-deleted stock-watchlist rows, and ticked right after every hourly calendar pass (and by the 10-minute monitor, and listed in Diagnostics). It goes down when a ticker has: a fetch error that is a 404 (permanent, at once) or has lasted 3 consecutive hourly runs (a transient 429 or network blip does not alert); no calendar row at all; or a DATE_KNOWN / DATE_ESTIMATED date that is already in the past. NOT_APPLICABLE and NOT_ANNOUNCED are genuine states and never alert. It is not judged until some row has been updated in the last 6 hours, so a fresh deploy or a stopped job is not reported as missing dates. The monitor's own rules give one message when it goes down naming the tickers and states (at most eight per group), a reminder every 24 hours while it lasts, one recovery message, and nothing when clean. (2) The daily digest (`monitor.CalendarDigest`, 07:30 UTC) lists report-date changes of the last 24 hours for owned and watchlist tickers (changed date, new date found after a report, or date gone), marks estimates, ignores thesis-only tickers, is sent at most once per UTC day (table `ticker_calendar_digest_sent`, migration 0069, so a restart does not repeat it) and sends nothing when there is nothing to say. First population of a ticker and the backfill are not changes. (3) Sender: the same one SC-55 settled on, the monitor's Telegram sender (`FT_TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN` / `FT_TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID` in `/etc/ft/env`), not `/api/bot/alerts` as the handover named. Checked on Jarvis on 2026-11-07 without printing any secret: its bot token and chat id equal those in `/etc/ft-bot/env` (hash and string comparison), it is `@FinsFTAlerts_bot` (id 8955129725), and `getChat` on the chat succeeds and reports a private chat; no test message was sent. Tests (scratch DB, fake notifier): a clean day is silent; an injected 404 gives one alert, none on the next hour, one recovery message, none afterwards; a 429 needs three runs; a past date shown as next and a ticker with no row are reported; not judged before the job has run; the digest is sent once, ignores thesis-only tickers and is silent on a quiet day; the full job-to-alert path with a fake source; each rule was kill-tested. Migration: 0069. Not changed: backfill report (P5). Reference updated: §3, §4, §5. Supersedes v1.104.0.

> **Overhaul:** 2026-11-07 (one hundred and twenty-first pass) — **SC-56 P5: backfill run and verification (v1.106.0).** (1) The per-run page budget is split: next-date lookups (a passed or missing date) always come first and the \"last reported date\" history lookups for thesis tickers run afterwards, in a second phase, with whatever is left of the 8-page budget and at most 3 (before, they shared one queue and could crowd each other out). (2) `ft calendar-refresh --second-per-run N` raises the cap for the one-off backfill after a deploy (the hourly job keeps 8). `ft calendar-report` prints the per-ticker table from the store: ownership, provider symbol, next date (\"est\" for estimates), state (a past date shown as next is flagged STALE), source, last reported date and the error or not-applicable reason. (3) Verification on a scratch copy of the live DB with live Yahoo, one run, started from the deployed command: `ft calendar-refresh --second-per-run 40` took 34 seconds for 63 tickers: 59 answered by Yahoo, 0 failed, 0 not reached, states DATE_ESTIMATED 40, DATE_KNOWN 18, NOT_APPLICABLE 4 (GLD, SLV, WBA, DJTWW), REPORTED 1 (VST, which reported on 2026-11-06; Yahoo has no next date yet). No ticker was MISSING, STALE, NOT_ANNOUNCED or FETCH_ERROR; all 16 holdings and every watchlist stock have a future date or NOT_APPLICABLE; the seven stale holdings (AEM, ALB, CCJ, LLY, LSCC, PAAS, RHM.DE) rolled to 2027-02-11, 2027-02-10, 2027-02-12, 2027-02-03, 2027-02-08, 2027-02-24 and 2027-03-10; BESI resolved through BESI.AS (2027-02-25) and BYD through 1211.HK (2027-04-01); SU.PA and RR.L, which Yahoo's calendar leaves empty, got 2027-02-18 and 2027-02-25 from the page. Known limit: for SU.PA and RR.L the page lists no recent reported row (its newest are 2020-07-28 and 2016-07-27), so their last reported date is old and the revision nudge would miss a report after the lock; a longer-term fix would take the reported date from another field. With the default budget of 8 the same convergence takes about four hourly runs. Nothing was written to the live DB and no alert was sent. Migrations: none. Reference updated: §4. Supersedes v1.105.0.

> **Overhaul:** 2026-11-08 (one hundred and twenty-second pass) — **SC-56 follow-ups: last-reported backfill from the quote endpoint, second-source failure alert, demo-mode masks (v1.107.0).** (1) Follow-up A: the earnings page can lack a recent reported row (SU.PA and RR.L showed 2020 and 2016, which would let `revision_needed` miss a report after the lock). A last-reported date that is missing or older than 220 days is now backfilled from `v7/finance/quote` (`earningsTimestamp`, `earningsTimestampStart`, `earningsTimestampEnd`): the latest of them that has passed, only if newer than the stored one, never touching the next date. It runs after the page history so it only asks for tickers still stale, at most once a day per ticker (a failed read is retried after two hours) and at most 20 a run, and it costs no second-source page budget. (2) Follow-up B: every second-source (earnings page) failure is stored per ticker (`second_error`, `second_error_count`, migration 0070), listed in the hourly summary line (`second_failed`, with a warning per ticker) and cleared by a successful read. A new monitor check `report_dates_second` goes down when at least 3 tickers have failed twice in a row, which is what a Yahoo layout change looks like (one or two failures are a bad hour and stay silent); same dedup, daily reminder and recovery as the other checks. (3) Demo mode: `/api/watchlist` is already empty in demo mode, so the new watchlist Report column is not served; holdings keep their calendar (public market data on tickers the masked book already shows); the Theses rows now also drop `earningsStatus`, `earningsSource`, `earningsFetchedAt`, `lastReportedDate` and `earningsNote` in demo mode (the SC-55 mask already dropped ownership, next date and urgency); Diagnostics hides the detail text of the `report_dates` checks, which name real holdings and watchlist tickers, in the monitor rows and the recent events. The report-date alerts and the 07:30 digest run on real data and go to the private Telegram chat, like every other monitor alert; there is no demo data set. Tests: backfill from past dates only, daily limit and two-hour retry, no regression to an older date, no lookup for a fresh ticker, failure stored, counted and cleared, the check silent for blips and alerting once for a layout change with a recovery message, and the demo masks; each rule was mutation-checked. Behaviour: job, monitor, API masks. Migration: 0070. Reference updated: §3, §5. Supersedes v1.106.0.

> **Overhaul:** 2026-11-08 (one hundred and twenty-third pass) — **SC-56b: one daily re-score summary instead of per-holding nudges (v1.108.0).** The bot's proactive poll (13, 17 and 21 UTC on weekdays) used to return one `thesis_revision_needed` alert per held thesis with `revision_needed`, repeated every day until the thesis was re-locked. The bot renders only RED and AMBER alerts, so those alerts produced an empty "FT alerts" message and were then acknowledged. (1) `/api/bot/alerts` no longer emits `thesis_revision_needed`. (2) `monitor.RevisionSummary` sends ONE message a day at 07:35 UTC through the monitor's Telegram sender (the chat @FinsFTAlerts_bot uses): the held tickers whose thesis has `revision_needed`, longest wait first, each with the report date, the days since it and the lock date (at most 25 lines, the rest counted). It is silent when the list is empty, is recorded in `notification_log` (holding_kind `system`, alert_kind `thesis_revision_summary`) so a restart or a second schedule does not repeat it, and a failed send is not recorded so the next call retries. The Theses tab and `get_theses` are unchanged: `revision_needed` still shows there for every live thesis. No migration. Tests: only held tickers and only locked theses are listed, ordering and days since report, silent when empty or re-scored, one message per day and again the next day, a failed send retried, the cap, and the bot endpoint no longer carrying the alert with a configured thesis engine; each rule was mutation-checked. A separate pass confirms that no path moves a last-reported date backwards (quote, page history, page read, a passed date still returned), with tests. Behaviour: bot alerts, monitor. Migrations: none. Reference updated: §5. Supersedes v1.107.0.

> **Overhaul:** 2026-11-08 (one hundred and twenty-fourth pass) — **SC-57: 52-week range, all-time high and market cap on the watchlist (v1.109.0).** Asked by ai for the AUR work: `/api/watchlist` and the MCP `get_watchlist` tool had price and analyst targets but no range or size data. (1) Migration 0071 adds `ticker_market_stats` (one row per stock-watchlist ticker). (2) `market.FetchYahooMarketStats` makes one `v8/chart?range=max&interval=1mo` call and one `price` module call. The 52-week high/low are read from the chart meta (never computed); the all-time high is the highest monthly high in the history Yahoo returned, stored with the month it fell in and the first and last bar of that history, so a young listing shows a short range; market cap comes from the price module. Nothing is estimated: a value Yahoo does not return stays null. (3) `refresh.RefreshMarketStats` runs in the 04:00 daily job and as `ft market-stats-refresh`, symbol via `ticker_symbol_map`, 250 ms apart. Each half (chart, market cap) is replaced only when it answered, so a failed fetch keeps the stored values and records the error. (4) Every `/api/watchlist` row now has `marketStats` with `high52w`, `low52w`, `ath` (+ `date`, `historyFrom`, `historyTo`) and `marketCap`, each `{value, source:"yahoo", asOf}`; crypto rows and rows not yet fetched are all-null with a `note`, never an error; a note flags an all-time high below the 52-week high (history under a year). The MCP tool reuses the handler, so it gets the same block; demo mode is unchanged (the route answers an empty list). A live run on a scratch copy of the database covered all 23 stock-watchlist tickers with no failures (AUR: 52-week 8.565/3.6, all-time high 17.77 from history 2021-05-10 to 2026-11-06, market cap about $8.9B). Reference updated: §3, §4, §5, §11. Supersedes v1.108.0.

> **Overhaul:** 2026-11-08 (one hundred and twenty-fifth pass) — **SC-57b: Robotics & Embodiment adapter name routes to its own slug (v1.110.0).** The parser had no entry for the Robotics & Embodiment adapter, so a thesis with that header could not be routed. `adapterAliases` now maps "Robotics & Embodiment", "Robotics and Embodiment", "Robotics/Embodiment", "Robotics / Embodiment", "Robotics-Embodiment", "Robotics Embodiment" and "robotics_embodiment" to the folder slug `robotics_embodiment`; the scorecard code stays the hyphenated `robotics-embodiment` (same underscore/hyphen pairing as `grid_storage_integrator` and `grid-storage-integrator`). Aliases only, no keyword route, so "Industrial Automation" and "Design Automation" are not swallowed (tested). The scorecard row is unchanged: status `needs-review`, not locked; locking waits for Fin to confirm the adapter supplement. Migrations: none. Reference updated: none. Supersedes v1.109.0.

> **Overhaul:** 2026-11-10 (one hundred and twenty-sixth pass) — **SC-58: sector series split repair, watchlist price refresh, Robotics & Embodiment locked (v1.111.0).** From the 2026-11-09 browser review (FT-081, FT-082). (1) FT-081: `sector_snapshots` stores the close seen on the day it was ingested, so when an ETF splits the older rows keep the pre-split price. SOXX and ITA both split 3:1 on 2026-11-05 (SOXX 450.41 to 131.90, ITA 212.84 to 68.11), and the 1W and 3M returns and the ratio vs SPY compared across the split (-71%, -74%, -67%), which drove the "rotating out" tags on the 8 sectors that use those two ETFs. `sector_rotation.FindStep` flags a one-day close ratio outside 0.6 to 1.7. `RepairSplits` re-reads Yahoo's split-adjusted daily history for any ETF with a step and replaces each stored close that differs by more than 0.5% (weekend and holiday rows, which carry the last close forward, take the latest adjusted bar on or before the date). It runs at the end of every daily ingest and as `ft sector-repair` (dry run unless `--apply`). `ComputeAll` shows `no_data` for a series that still has a step, never a tag. On a copy of the live DB the repair changed 4,728 rows and left no step. (2) FT-082: `watchlist.current_price` is a one-time quote taken when the row is added (AVGO on 2026-05-16, BE, MOD and CAT on 2026-05-20), and nothing refreshed it, so "in range" used month-old prices. Migration 0072 adds `price` and `price_as_of` to `ticker_market_stats`, filled from the chart meta in the existing daily stats pass. `/api/watchlist` shows that price as `currentPrice` with `priceAsOf` and computes `inRange` from it; the stored quote is the fallback and is never overwritten. This also fills rows that had no price. (3) The `robotics-embodiment` scorecard (version 1) was set from `needs-review` to `locked` on 2026-11-10 by Fin's approval of the Robotics & Embodiment v1.1 lock supplement: four sub-types (humanoid, industrial-automation, autonomous-systems, robotics-enabler); classify by where the thesis value sits; pre-revenue names scored on the same /16 scale. The adapter is recorded as locked. Supersedes v1.110.0.

> **Overhaul:** 2026-11-10 (one hundred and twenty-seventh pass) — **SC-59: circuit breaker armed in the nightly job, advisory, cash-inclusive (v1.112.0).** From the 2026-11-09 browser review (FT-079, FT-110) and Fin's FT-106 decision that the breaker stays advisory. (1) The drawdown is now measured on the portfolio value INCLUDING cash (as the Summary donut is): each day's value plus that day's recorded cash, and today's value plus today's cash. Migration 0073 adds `cash_value_usd` to `portfolio_value_history`; days recorded before it use today's cash balance (cash history was not kept), which the card states. On the live data this reads about -17.6% (peak 29,865 + 13,869 on 2026-09-23 against 22,158 + 13,869), against -25.81% excluding cash. In demo mode cash is excluded so the real balance cannot reach the view. (2) `Server.RunNightlyRiskCheck` runs at 04:30 UTC, after the 04:00 job has written the day's value row: it stores cash on that row, then applies the breaker. Before, the breaker only armed inside the manual `POST /api/risk/snapshot` route, so the nightly job never armed it and the card read NORMAL beside a -25.81% drawdown. (3) The breaker is ADVISORY: arming sets `risk_circuit_breaker_active` and `risk_circuit_breaker_until` (14 days) and sends ONE Telegram message on the transition to armed ("FT risk (ADVISORY): drawdown ... Advisory only: no trade is blocked."); no further message while it stays armed. `PortfolioRiskCheck.AllowsNewTrade` is not wired into any trade flow. Auto-clear is unchanged (drawdown above -5% and the 14 days passed). (4) The Settings card shows "(ADVISORY)" next to the state, "BREACH, not yet armed" when the drawdown is beyond the cap but the flag is not set, and the basis under the drawdown figure. Behaviour: job, API, UI. Migration: 0073. Supersedes v1.111.0.
